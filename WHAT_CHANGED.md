## In one paragraph

The old Lab 03 told the learner that "greedy gets stuck at AB|CDE cut 5" and used random seeds whose
cuts disagreed between the examples file, the transcript and the lab. The upgrade fixes one exact rule per
method, checks all of it with a reference script, and rebuilds the deck, narration, browser lab and quiz on those
numbers. On the tiny graph the clean greedy rule now gives **ABC|DE cut 3**, which is the best legal split.

## New concepts (what the learner now has to understand)

1. **A starting split must be legal and report two numbers.** Every node on exactly one side, no side above
   ceil(n/2) nodes; the report is cutsize and balance, never cutsize alone.
2. **Each constructor is a short exact rule.**
   - Random: seeded shuffle, deal floor(n/2) nodes to side 0.
   - Greedy: anchor on the heaviest edge, then put each node on the side where it adds less cut, under the size cap, ties by smaller name.
   - Grow: start from a chosen node and repeatedly take the heaviest edge leaving the grown set until half the nodes are in.
3. **The start decides how much work refinement has left.** On the tiny graph the cut ranges from 3 to 16 over
   random seeds 0–15 (3 of 16 seeds hit 3); grow from D or E gives ABC|DE (3), grow from A or B gives AB|CDE (5).
4. **Ties and size limits change the answer.** Shown on three extra graphs: *star* (no size cap gives an illegal 5|1
   split, cut 5; the capped rule gives 3|3, cut 15), *tie* (greedy 9, grow from C finds 4) and *bridge* (greedy 12, grow
   from A or B finds the optimum 4). A good habit follows: try several starts and keep the best legal one.

## Polish pass: shorter, in five parts

The first version repeated itself and played as one 8-minute block. The second pass changed:

- **Repetition removed.** Each idea is said once: "try several starts, keep the best" (opening, slide 18, recap), cut and balance
  (slide 4), "KL and FM need a legal split" (slide 6). The long video went from 8:31 to **7:26 in English and 7:55 in French**.
- **Five parts, each opening with a hook and ending with "Pause and guess".** 1 What and why · 2 Three ways to start ·
  3 One start is not enough · 4 Three traps · 5 Your turn. The next part answers the question the last one asked.
- **A new picture every 5 to 10 seconds where it helps.** Nine step figures, each the full figure with its later parts hidden
  (`chip-to-graph-1`, `cut-and-balance-1`, `worked-unlucky-1`/`-2`, `grow-a-1`/`-2`, `star-cap-1`, `tie-graph-1`,
  `bridge-graph-1`), so the picture builds up while the narration goes on. The deck has 30 slides (was 22).
- **The five walkthrough frames lost their spoilers** (no "seed 3" answer, no grow-A result before it is shown); the last one asks
  "All three found cut 3. Was that luck?". Text in `assets/STEPS.md` and `i18n/fr/walkthrough-steps.json`.
- **French follows every change** (same 30 slides, same parts).

## Findings for the teacher

None of these were edited, because they are outside the two allowed folders.

1. **Wrong seed-7 claim in `platform/assets/partitioning-core.js`** (docstring of `randomBipartition`, line 87): it says
   seed 7 on the tiny graph gives AE|BCD, cut 12. Running the function gives AB|CDE, cut 5. The function also shuffles
   with a comparator, so its result depends on the node order. `greedyInitialBipartition` has no size cap and its forced
   node depends on the letters (renaming A↔E, B↔D changes its answer).
2. **`courses/learn_partitioning/scripts/enrich_transcripts.py`** (lines 32–33) writes the same claim ("seed seven's random
   bipartition matches the familiar bad seed shape AE versus BCD with cutsize twelve") into the transcript. Do not re-run
   it on this module, or the claim comes back.
3. **Missing `<` in `platform/tools/initial-bipartition/index.html`**: the first line was `!DOCTYPE html>`, which printed the
   text on the page and put it in quirks mode. Fixed here with one byte. The sibling clone `eda-learning` has the same bug.
4. **Lab 12 and the multistart code still use the old random and greedy.** `platform/assets/partition-benchmark-app.js` calls
   `randomBipartition(nodes, opts.randomSeed ?? 7)` and `greedyInitialBipartition` (lines 198, 215, 242, 258), so their
   numbers differ from the new tables. `EXAMPLES.md` says so in one sentence. This clone has no Lab 12 page, so "Lab 12" is
   as stated by the user.
5. **The walkthrough viewer still holds the old frames.** `platform/tools/algorithm-walkthrough/walkthrough.js` (not in this
   clone; checked in `../eda-learning`) still shows "Random seed=1: lucky ABC|DE", seed=4 → cut 13 and "Greedy initial:
   AB|CDE cut 5". The new PNGs and the new text for the five frames are in `assets/STEPS.md`; the viewer page will disagree
   with the deck until the new block is copied into `walkthrough.js`. The polish pass changed that text again (frame 1 title
   "Three ways to build a first split", no seed-3 or grow-A spoilers, frame 5 "All three found cut 3. Was that luck?"), so a
   block copied earlier must be replaced with the current one.
